# Dataflow of the Medical Clinic Database System

This document outlines the lifecycle of data within the Medical Clinic Database System, explaining how data enters the system, the paths it takes between tables, and the final outputs generated.

## 1. Data Entry Point (Registration)
Data enters the system primarily through the Receptionist or Admin (`USERS` table). When a new patient arrives at the clinic:
- The receptionist enters the patient's demographics (Name, DOB, Gender, Contact) into the **`PATIENTS`** table.
- This serves as the foundational data required before any medical service can be rendered.

## 2. Core Operational Flow (The Visit)
The core operational logic centers around the **`VISITS`** table, which acts as the main bridge connecting people to services:
- **Scheduling/Check-In**: The receptionist creates a new record in the `VISITS` table, assigning the `patient_id` to a specific `doctor_id` (from the `DOCTORS` table) and recording the `visit_date`.
- **Examination**: The doctor examines the patient and updates the `VISITS` record with a `diagnosis`. 

## 3. Dependent Data Streams (Services & Prescriptions)
During or after the examination, the data branches into two dependent streams:
- **Prescriptions**: If the doctor prescribes medication, records are inserted into the **`PRESCRIPTIONS`** table. Each prescription is strictly linked back to the specific `visit_id`.
- **Medical Services**: If the doctor orders tests (e.g., X-Rays, Blood Tests), the system reads available services from the **`MEDICAL_SERVICES`** catalog and logs the performed services into the **`VISIT_SERVICES`** junction table, linking `visit_id` to `service_id`.

## 4. Financial Processing (Billing)
Once the visit concludes, data flows into the financial stage:
- The system reads all `service_id` records linked to the `visit_id` from the `VISIT_SERVICES` table.
- It looks up the `price` for each service from `MEDICAL_SERVICES`, calculates the total, and generates a record in the **`BILLS`** table.
- The receptionist later updates the `status` of the bill to "Paid".

## 5. Final Outputs
The structured data movement allows for several critical outputs:
1. **Patient Invoices**: Generated by querying the `BILLS`, `VISITS`, and `PATIENTS` tables.
2. **Medical History Reports**: Generated by querying a patient's past `VISITS`, `PRESCRIPTIONS`, and `VISIT_SERVICES`.
3. **Revenue/Operational Reports**: Admins can query `BILLS` to see daily revenue, or `VISITS` to evaluate doctor workloads.
